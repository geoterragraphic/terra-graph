Recently I ‘graduated’ from RevoU full-stack business development course that last for 3 months. I never imagine that an online course like this would have such a celebration. Even though it’s only online graduation, but there’s a lot of emotion, missing moments, great connections have made ever since. A few years ago, I was also ‘in touch’ at one of the top-notch online course, (clue, the brand colors are green and blue). But the feelings are way too far different. This could be the new breakthrough of learning experience in distance. Because we know that recently, everyone is experiencing online learning, and many of us are really bored of online learning.

So, we want to rethink a new way of learning today and in the future. Learning become much demanded because many new jobs and roles existed just recently, and many companies are defining the role of the job. For example, there’s a product manager that lies at the intersection between business, technology, and user experience. There are many definitions and differentiation of the product manager role. There’s that more technical, some are business-minded and some are very data-minded. There’s also a bias between product owner, product manager, and project manager, where at small scale companies, it’s been rough to differ.

According to the most recent survey from Babson Survey Research Group, over 30 percent of higher education students in the United States are taking at least one distance course. Online education is a sensible choice whether you’re a teenager or an adult. As a student, this can be a useful learning method for sharpening your skills in a difficult subject or learning a new skill. educations.com

Learning has been evolved from exclusive and institutional to more liberated and equal-minded. With the help of technological advancement, we can access many learning materials such as ebooks, youtube videos, and online courses. The learning activity has become affordable and accessible to anyone. This will be a threat to a conventional institution like a university, that hasn’t been evolving so much to match the industry needs (especially in Indonesia). Where there’s more and more tech company founded, there’s a lack of skilled talent to fulfill the industry demand. More of them are already acquired by the big unicorn startup and the rest are not so qualified.

So there’s lies an opportunity to fill the huge amount of demand for skilled talent. And if we only depended on the university, there will be a long time to wait for the talent to be ready. So the online course is the best way to do it. Faster, taught by industry leader and match with the industry need. There’s also a guarantee to job connection and made an online course more desirable to go rather than a university degree.

there was already high growth and adoption in education technology, with global edtech investments reaching US$18.66 billion in 2019 and the overall market for online education projected to reach $350 Billion by 2025. Whether it is language apps, virtual tutoring, video conferencing tools, or online learning software.

So, talk about online learning, there are several ways to do that. Some offer video-recorded materials (asynchronous) which are divided into modules and parts, combined with quizzes and exams to get the certificate. Some online learning offers a course with boot camp mode (synchronous), with intensive learning on a daily/weekly basis. There’s an assignment to test your knowledge and earn you a certificate in the end. There’s also online learning with webinar or webinar series method. With 1 to 7 days of the learning process. All of them are offer you a shareable certificate to prove that you have acquired the skills.

See the type of online learning from Fordham University https://www.fordham.edu/about/leadership-and-administration/administrative-offices/office-of-the-provost/provost-office-units/online-learning/types-of-online-learning/

These days, the company is more noticing the importance of course certificates, those are lifted you to stand from the crowd. Because the ordinary fresh graduate is not talent-ready enough. The online course certificate not only differentiates you from other candidates but also gives’s you a better perspective on the job role and work experience, so that makes you ready more than ever.

Job connections are also the advantage of online courses. Because that makes them different from traditional institutions. The course institution wants to make sure that your knowledge is ready enough and applicable as soon as possible to the work. So they make a partnership with other companies to become their hiring partner. This hiring partner can ‘test’ you with real cases, internship opportunities, or become an apprenticeship.

To conclude, there’s a new emerging market of online learning that is demanded by the tech industry. The talent must be ready enough to keep the pace of the current situation. Because there’s a lot of uncertainty and changing market. So the company is shifting to more advanced and agile to be relevant with the market.

It’s a fact that online learning is the future and will undoubtedly replace land-based learning in the future. Mobile platforms have given access to more people to benefit from online learning, and this has created huge data, then machine learning has given personalized solutions to the course content with using the big data. forbes.com
