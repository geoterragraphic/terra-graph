About us

Who we are

We are a digital marketing agency working with global clients across Malaysia, India, Saudi Arabia, the United States and Australia. We write meaningful content, manage digital channels and create marketing collateral that supports product marketing.

Terra Graph has served clients in the creative industry, agritech, startups, publishing, distribution, technology, healthcare and manufacturing. We work closely with cross-functional teams to keep a brand consistent across every digital platform, and we keep up with industry trends so our marketing stays data-driven and current.

AI Automation

Using Claude, we automate the repetitive work behind campaigns, reporting and content production, so your team spends its time on strategy and creative decisions.
